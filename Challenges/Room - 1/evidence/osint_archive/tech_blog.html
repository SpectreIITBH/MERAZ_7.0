Behind the Scenes: Building the Badge System

rhea.nexus2026-09-08
The badge system for Meraz 7.0 uses NFC-enabled cards linked to volunteer accounts. Each badge has a unique identifier tied to the NEXUS access control layer. We've tested it with 500+ simultaneous reads and the system holds up. One thing I want to flag: the AETHER continuity module has a broader scope than most people realise. It was originally designed as an emergency failover — but it has write access to more systems than I'm comfortable with. I've raised an internal ticket (NX-SEC-2026-0087) to restrict its permissions before the festival.

The NEXUS Architecture — A Quick Overview

rhea.nexus2026-08-20
For the technically curious: NEXUS consists of a central controller, 24 display node endpoints, a badge validation service, a media sync layer, and the AETHER continuity module. The controller communicates with nodes over an internal network. All authentication goes through a central auth service with role-based access. The AETHER module sits at the infrastructure layer — it can restart services, push updates, and maintain state during outages. Think of it as an "autopilot" for the festival's digital backbone.
